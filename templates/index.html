Protect an image before you share it.

CogniShield adds a change you can barely see that disrupts AI image-to-image models, checks that it survives JPEG compression, and signs the result so anyone can confirm it hasn't been altered.

  1. 1

    Perturb

    Seeded, edge-aware noise is added inside a strict per-pixel budget, so the change stays hard to see.

  2. 2

    Test

    The protected copy is recompressed at the JPEG qualities you pick, and CogniShield measures what survives.

  3. 3

    Sign

    The protected PNG is hashed and signed with your local Ed25519 key, so anyone can verify it later.

How strong should the protection be?

Stronger protection disrupts AI models more, but is more likely to leave a faint texture on flat areas like skies and walls. Balanced works for most photos.
